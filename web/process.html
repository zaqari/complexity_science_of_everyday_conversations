(b) Convergence-Information (CI / $I(x;y)$) for three turns by Speaker 1.

(c) Proportion of total information accrued for Speakers 1 and 2 by temporal distance in turns $\Delta_t$

Fig. 1: An example of the steps involved in our analysis. Utterances in figure (1.a) are numbered according to when they occur during a conversation, and $\Delta_t$ indicates how many turns are between a first subsequent utterance $y$ (the start of a time-series) and the next subsequent utterance $y$ (our measurement of interest with regards to power-law scaling). The utterance $x$ is compared to each utterance $y$ separately in order to calculate convergence information (CI) for each comparison. The bars in figure (1.b) capture CI for the utterance $x$ (printed on the y-axis) compared to subsequent utterances up until the end of a conversation. Figure (1.c) demonstrates the average proportion of information accrued as a function of $\Delta_t$ -- the number of turns separating the first utterance made by some speaker $sp(y)$ and another one -- for a total conversation, for both the speaker of x compared to themself (in gold) and one of their CPs (in blue).
